RenTR Phase 2.6 — Final Quality Architecture
Date: 2026-08-26

Primary goal
------------
Remove the remaining systematic Hybrid v3.1 benchmark failure classes before in-game testing.

1) Deterministic Ren'Py stutter renderer
---------------------------------------
Patterns such as:
  y--{w=0.5}you
  d--{w=0.5}don't
  n--{w=0.5}naked
  B--{w=0.5}but
  Y-{w=0.3}yes
are no longer sent to the LLM with an opaque wait placeholder embedded inside an English word.

RenTR now:
  a. normalizes the English stutter for translation,
  b. translates the semantic text,
  c. locates the Turkish semantic anchor,
  d. deterministically re-renders the original Ren'Py wait tag and hesitation pattern.

Ordinary/internal Ren'Py controls that are not recognized as this narrow stutter pattern remain
under the existing fail-closed protection rules.

2) Short semantic ambiguity gate
--------------------------------
New deterministic hard-risk checks cover benchmark escapes such as:
  I care -> Bana göre
  It's about time -> Artık yeter
  About me? -> Benden bahsetmek ister misin?
  Meet with Holly -> Holly ile tanışın
  hold my own -> kendi başımayım
  standalone Sex -> Cinsellik

The checks route these cases through repair/selection instead of accepting risk=0 output.

3) Explicit-language corrections
---------------------------------
- Fixed a detector bug where Turkish conjunction "ama" could accidentally satisfy the "am"
  anatomy check.
- "Cum in my pussy" may naturally become "İçime boşal"; Turkish does not need an awkward noun
  repetition when the sexual location is grammatically unambiguous.
- TranslateGemma and Aya prompts receive source-specific semantic guardrails earlier.
- Explicitness remains a fidelity criterion, not a reason to censor.

4) Stronger selector semantics
------------------------------
Qwen3 is explicitly instructed to derive source meaning first, then compare candidates.
Known idiom failure modes such as "hold my own", "pull strings" and "it's about time" are called
out as semantic traps. Surface similarity must not beat meaning.

5) Boundary punctuation QC
--------------------------
A new deterministic check rejects stray punctuation inserted immediately after a leading Ren'Py
control tag, e.g.:
  {image=...}This... -> {image=...}:Bu...
This routes the candidate to repair instead of silently accepting the artifact.

Local verification performed in the reconstruction environment
--------------------------------------------------------------

- focused regression suite covering pipeline, stutter, semantic repair and quality: 58 PASS
